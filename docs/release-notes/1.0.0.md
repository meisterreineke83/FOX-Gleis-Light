## Gleis 1.0 – trains, buses and your DB ticket on the Light Phone III

I wanted to leave my smartphone at home and still travel by train in Germany. So I built **Gleis**:
a small, calm tool for the Light Phone III that does two things – find a connection and show my
Deutsche Bahn ticket. Nothing else.

**What it does**
- Journey planner for all public transport in Germany (trains, long-distance buses, S-/U-Bahn,
  trams, buses) with delays and platforms
- Saves your latest results, so you can still look them up in a dead zone
- Fetches your DB tickets from a mailbox used only for train tickets and shows the barcode full
  screen, black on white, offline – it has already passed a real ticket inspection
- Tickets tidy themselves up 14 days after the journey

No account, no ads, no tracking. Open source (MIT).

**Install:** in Bright Market add `github.com/meisterreineke83/FOX-Gleis-Light`, or download
`FOX-Gleis-Light.apk` below. Setup is explained in the [README](https://github.com/meisterreineke83/FOX-Gleis-Light#readme).

The app is in German, as it's made for travelling in Germany. Not affiliated with Deutsche Bahn
or Light. Feedback and bug reports are very welcome – just open an issue.
